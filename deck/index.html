R3U|Phone Link

Your Android phone, in any browser on your Wi-Fi. Photos, notifications, messages, contacts and files, without a cable, a cloud or an account.

PresentationVersion 1.1.1 plus the new layoutAndroid 11 and newer
What it is

Use your phone from your computer, in three steps

1

Install the app

Download the signed APK from the landing page and install it on the phone. Approve "Install unknown apps" once.

2

Unlock and set a passcode

Allow photo access, confirm with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, then choose a web passcode. The server starts by itself.

3

Open the link

Scan the QR code or type the link in a browser on the same Wi-Fi. Enter the passcode and your phone appears.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Everything travels from the phone to your browser over your own network.

How a session starts

From opening the app to seeing your phone

PHONE 1Allow access

Full photo access ("Allow all"). Partial access is refused with an explanation.

PHONE 2Unlock

Fingerprint, face or screen lock on every launch. No screen lock, no server.

PHONE 3Web passcode

Set once. Stored only as a salted hash on the phone.

PHONE 4Server starts

A random token is created. The link and QR code appear.

COMPUTER 1Open the link

The token becomes a protected cookie and disappears from the address bar.

COMPUTER 2Enter the passcode

Five wrong tries lock that computer out for a while.

COMPUTER 3Use your phone

Only the features you switched on in the app are available.

ANYTIMEStop

Stop server in the app or the notification. Wi-Fi off also stops sharing.

On the phone

The app has a few screens you will use

Photos

Your whole library, by album

  • Albums come from the folders on the phone: Camera, Screenshots, WhatsApp Images and more.
  • Handles 10,000+ photos; tested with 12,000.
  • Grouped by day, with correct aspect ratios.
  • Smart filters: Screenshots, Large files over 5 MB.
  • Search by name, sort, and make the thumbnails larger or smaller.

Screenshots use sample data, not real photos.

Photos

Select, download or move to trash

One photo downloads as a file; several stream as a zip straight to disk. Trashed photos stay in Android's trash for 30 days.

Photos

A proper photo viewer

  • Previous and next by button, arrow keys or swipe.
  • Zoom 50% to 400%: buttons, wheel, pinch, or + and -.
  • Double-click to toggle zoom; drag to pan.
  • "3 of 1,284", file name and a Download button.
Alerts

Phone notifications on your computer

  • Apps with notifications are listed on the left, with counts.
  • Dismiss one or all; it clears on the phone too.
  • Reply to chat apps that offer a reply action.
  • Needs Notification access, switched on once in Android settings.
  • The badge on the app bar shows how many are waiting.
Messages

Read and send text messages

  • Conversations on the left, with contact names.
  • Full history for the open conversation.
  • Send from the browser, delivered from the phone's number.
  • New message to any number from the ribbon.
  • Needs SMS permission; off by default.

Known limit: Android records sent texts only for the default SMS app, so a reply sent here may not appear in history later.

Contacts

Look up anyone, then message or call

  • A to Z list with search by name or number.
  • Every number and email, with a Copy button.
  • Send message jumps straight into a new text.
  • Call on phone opens the phone's dialer with the number filled in.
  • Needs contacts permission; off by default.
Files and clipboard

Move files and text both ways

  • Drag files onto the page: they land in the phone's Download/R3U Phone Link folder.
  • Share a file from any app to R3U | Phone Link: it appears here to download.
  • Clipboard: send text to the phone, or share text from the phone to the computer.
  • No broad storage permission needed.
Phone

Status and Find my phone

  • Device name, model and Android version.
  • Battery level and charging state, also in the status bar.
  • Storage used and free.
  • Find my phone: rings at full volume for up to a minute, even on silent.
Open on the phone

Send a link, a number or a place to the phone

  • Open a web link in the phone's browser.
  • Open the dialer with a number ready.
  • Show a place or address in the phone's maps.
  • If the app is open it happens at once; otherwise a notification appears for you to tap.
  • You then use it on the phone itself. This is not screen mirroring.
Any screen

Works on a laptop, a tablet or another phone

Security

Locked by default, in layers

Random link token

New for every server start; only shown in the app.

Protected cookie

HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict. Every request needs it.

Passcode with lockout

Hashed on the phone. Five wrong tries, then 30 seconds, doubling.

Feature switches

Sensitive features are off until you switch them on. Enforced on the phone, not just hidden.

Local network only

Devices outside private address ranges are refused.

Unlock to start

Fingerprint, face or screen lock every time the app is opened.

Honest limit: traffic is plain HTTP on your Wi-Fi, so use a network you trust. HTTPS is a later project.

Straight talk

What it does not do yet

Not on the internet yet

Phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi.

No app mirroring yet

Installed apps can't run in a browser. Mirroring the screen is a separate, later project.

Plain HTTP on the LAN

Passcode and content can be read by someone sniffing your Wi-Fi.

Sideloaded app

Not on the Play Store, because SMS and notification access would be rejected there.
